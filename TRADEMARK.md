The code in this repository is MIT licensed: use it, change it, ship it, sell things built with it.

The **tesserai** name and logo are not part of that license. They identify the official product at [tesserai.design](https://tesserai.design) and the official `@tesserai` packages on npm.

**You may:**

- say truthfully that your project uses, works with, or is based on tesserai ("built with tesserai", "a fork of tesserai");
- use the name in articles, talks, tutorials and reviews;
- keep the name in the code and license notices you received.

**Please don't:**

- name a fork, product, package, domain or company "tesserai", or something confusingly close to it;
- use the logo, or a look-alike, for your own product;
- suggest that your project is official or endorsed by tesserai when it isn't.

If you're unsure, or want permission for something not covered here, open an issue and ask.
